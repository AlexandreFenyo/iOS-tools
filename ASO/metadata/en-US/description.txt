HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick one of the ready-made floor plans, or use a photo of your own plan
2. Walk to a spot, tap once — the app measures throughput and latency there
3. Repeat for each room. The heat map builds as you go
4. Save the finished map to your photos, to compare after you move the router

Step-by-step mode does the setup for you: no target to choose, no protocol to
configure, no documentation to read. The advanced interface is still there when
you want full control.

BEFORE YOU BUY — WHAT THIS APP DOES NOT DO
Apple does not let any third-party iOS app read WiFi signal strength or scan
neighboring networks, and no App Store app can do it — this is why professional
tools need external hardware. This app maps what it can measure at the spot where
you are standing: real throughput and latency. That is what a home heat map needs.
It measures the network you are connected to, one heat map at a time.
